## Offline negative-path validation

No paid failure was intentionally triggered. Offline tests cover:

- missing variables;
- hostile or mismatched Model Studio URLs;
- status categories for 400, 401, 402, 403, 404, 422, 429, 500 and 503;
- separation of authentication, billing, parameters, rate limit and Provider failures;
- output that never includes a simulated `SECRET_TEST_VALUE`.

DeepSeek-specific documented categories are 400, 401, 402, 422, 429, 500 and 503. Generic 403/404 handling remains defensive and is not described as a Provider guarantee. Bailian uses its own Region/Workspace/model guidance rather than copying DeepSeek semantics.
